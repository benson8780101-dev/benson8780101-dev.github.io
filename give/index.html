We are sent and cared for by YWAM Taiwan, our sending organization. Your gift goes to them first, and they pass it on to us. It takes about 3 minutes. 我們由差會 YWAM 台灣青年使命團差派和照顧。你的奉獻會先進到差會，再由差會轉給我們。大約 3 分鐘就能完成。

Where your gift goes奉獻會到哪裡

Your gift goes to YWAM Taiwan, not to a personal account. YWAM Taiwan is part of Youth With A Mission, an international mission movement, and has served in Taiwan for over 30 years. They keep the records and pass support on to us.你的奉獻是進到 YWAM 台灣青年使命團，不是進到個人帳戶。YWAM 台灣是國際青年使命團（Youth With A Mission）的一份子，在台灣服事超過 30 年，由他們記帳，再把支持轉給我們。

On the card page you'll see the recipient 社團法人青年使命團. That is YWAM Taiwan's registered name in Taiwan.在刷卡頁面上，你會看到收款單位是 社團法人青年使命團，也就是 YWAM 台灣在台灣登記的正式名稱。

About YWAM Taiwan →認識 YWAM 台灣 → Their official giving page →差會官方奉獻頁 →
Our staff code. Add it to every gift.我們的同工代碼，每次奉獻都要寫
TS2502
Without this code, your gift goes to YWAM Taiwan's general fund instead of to us.沒寫代碼的話，奉獻會變成差會的一般經費，不會轉給我們。

Two ways to give. Pick the one that fits you.兩種奉獻方式，選適合你的就好。

Give by card, from anywhere刷卡奉獻（任何國家都可以）

Works with any credit or debit card: Visa, Mastercard or JCB. In the US and don't need a tax receipt? This is the easiest way.信用卡、簽帳金融卡都可以（Visa、Mastercard、JCB）。在美國、但不需要抵稅收據的朋友，用這個方法最簡單。

  1. Open YWAM Taiwan's secure card page (run by ECPay, a Taiwanese payment company). Amounts are in Taiwan dollars (NT$). Your card converts them for you. NT$1,000 is roughly €30 or US$32.打開 YWAM 台灣的刷卡頁面（由台灣金流公司綠界 ECPay 處理）。金額是新台幣，你的卡會自動換算。新台幣 1,000 元大約是 30 歐元或 32 美元。
    Open card page →打開刷卡頁面 →
  2. In the Remarks box, write在 Remarks（備註）欄寫上 TS2502, your full name你的全名
    Please write your own name, not ours. For example: TS2502, Tom Smith請寫你自己的名字，不是我們的名字。例如：TS2502, 王大明
    That's all. The code tells YWAM Taiwan the gift is for us, so you don't need to send anything else.這樣就完成了。差會看到代碼就知道是給我們的，不用再另外通知。
    Want to give every month? Under payment method, choose 定期定額 (recurring) and pick how often.想每月固定奉獻？付款方式選 定期定額，再選多久扣一次。

A 3.5% card fee comes out of the gift. US cards: a card with no foreign transaction fee saves you a little more.國外的卡會從奉獻金額扣 3.5% 手續費。美國朋友用「免國外交易手續費」的卡會更省。

Prefer a bank transfer? International wire details are coming soon. Reply to our newsletter, and we'll send them to you.想用銀行匯款嗎？國際匯款資料還在準備中。回覆我們的代禱信，我們會寄給你。
