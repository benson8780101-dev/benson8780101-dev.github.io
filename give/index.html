We are sent and cared for by YWAM Taiwan, our sending organization. Your gift goes to them first, and they pass it on to us. It takes about 3 minutes. 我們由差會 YWAM 台灣青年使命團差派和照顧。你的奉獻會先進到差會，再由差會轉給我們。大約 3 分鐘就能完成。

Our staff code. Add it to every gift.我們的同工代碼，每次奉獻都要寫
TS2502
Without this code, your gift goes to YWAM Taiwan's general fund instead of to us.沒寫代碼的話，奉獻會變成差會的一般經費，不會轉給我們。

Give from the US從美國奉獻

  1. Open the YWAM Montana giving page. YWAM Montana is a US partner that receives gifts for YWAM Taiwan.打開 YWAM Montana 奉獻頁面。YWAM Montana 是在美國幫 YWAM 台灣代收奉獻的夥伴機構。
    Open giving page →打開奉獻頁面 →
  2. Under Designated For, choose在 Designated For 選 YWAM Taiwan
  3. Fill in your details and card or bank account, then press Donate. You can give once or monthly. YWAM Montana emails you a US tax receipt.填好你的資料和信用卡或銀行帳戶，按 Donate。可以選單次或每月固定奉獻。YWAM Montana 會寄美國抵稅收據給你。
  4. The US form has no place for our code, so please send YWAM Taiwan a short email after you give. Include TS2502, your name, the amount and the date. That way your gift reaches us.美國的表單沒有地方寫我們的代碼，所以奉獻完請寄一封簡短的信給 YWAM 台灣，寫上 TS2502、你的名字、金額和日期，奉獻才會轉給我們。
    donations.ywamtw@gmail.com

Give from Europe or elsewhere從歐洲或其他國家奉獻

  1. Open YWAM Taiwan's secure card page (run by ECPay, a Taiwanese payment company). Amounts are in Taiwan dollars (NT$). Your card converts them for you. NT$1,000 is roughly €30 or US$32.打開 YWAM 台灣的刷卡頁面（由台灣金流公司綠界 ECPay 處理）。金額是新台幣，你的卡會自動換算。新台幣 1,000 元大約是 30 歐元或 32 美元。
    Open card page →打開刷卡頁面 →
  2. In the notes field, write在備註欄寫上 TS2502, your full name你的全名
  3. After you give, fill in this short form so YWAM Taiwan can match your gift to us and send you a receipt.奉獻完填一下這份簡短表單，差會才能把奉獻對到我們，並寄收據給你。
    Open the form →打開表單 →

Card companies charge a 3.5% fee, which comes out of the gift.國外信用卡會扣 3.5% 手續費，從奉獻金額裡扣。
